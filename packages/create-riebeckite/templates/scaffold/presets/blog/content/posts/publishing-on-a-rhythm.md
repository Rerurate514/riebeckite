## Pick a cadence you can keep

Weekly, fortnightly, or monthly all work. What matters is that the interval survives a busy week, because a gap you can predict is easier to trust than one you cannot.

## Bank drafts instead of chasing deadlines

Keep two finished drafts ahead of the schedule. When a week goes badly, the queue carries the post and you keep the rhythm without publishing something half-read.

## Stop at good enough

A post that ships teaches you more than a draft that waits for permission. Publish, then read the piece as a stranger would and fix what actually confused them. Return to [[writing-a-useful-post]] when the next draft starts from scratch.
