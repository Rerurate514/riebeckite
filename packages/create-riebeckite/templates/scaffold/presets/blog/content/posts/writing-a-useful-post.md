## Start with the reader

Name the person you are writing for and what they already know. A post that tries to serve everyone tends to help no one.

## Keep one claim

One post, one argument. Everything else either supports it or belongs in another draft.

## Cut the rest

Read the draft once for structure. If a paragraph does not move the claim forward, remove it. Continue with [[editing-before-publishing]].
